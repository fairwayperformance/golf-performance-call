Your Self-Assessment

Run Through These 4 Mobility Tests

Watch each video, follow along, and note where you feel limited. The test you struggle with most is the one we want to talk about on your call.

1

Thoracic Rotation Test

Tests upper-back mobility — the rotation engine of your swing. Limited here caps your turn, kills clubhead speed, and forces the lower back to compensate (and complain).

2

Shoulder External Rotation Test

Tests how far your shoulders can rotate externally — the move that loads the backswing. Limited here means a shortened turn, lost speed, and inconsistent strike.

3

Internal Hip Rotation Test

Tests your hips' ability to rotate inward — critical for the downswing. Limited internal rotation is one of the biggest hidden causes of lower-back pain in golfers over 35.

4

Dissociation Tests

Tests your ability to move upper body and lower body independently. Poor dissociation means hips and shoulders fire together — power leaks out, consistency falls apart.
